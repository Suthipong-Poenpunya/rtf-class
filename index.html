01 / Learn

What is R-T-F?

A clear AI prompt tells the AI who to be, what to do, and how to show the answer.

R

Role

Tell AI who to be.

Act as a Grade 11 teacher.
T

Task

Tell AI what to do.

Explain High Performance Computing in simple English.
F

Format

Tell AI how to present the answer.

Present the answer in a table.
02 / Compare

Bad prompt vs. clear prompt

Notice how specific instructions make the answer easier to use.

✕ Vague prompt
“Explain what HPC is.”

The answer may be too long or too advanced.

✓ Clear R-T-F prompt
“Act as a Grade 11 computer science teacher. Explain High Performance Computing in simple English. Present the answer in a table.”

The role, task, and format are all specified.

Tip: AI responses can vary. Always check the accuracy of the information.
